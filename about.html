ABOUT ERABITA

Curious
by nature.

Erabita is an independent general-interest magazine from Japan. We write about the things that shape everyday choices — the technology we use, the places we visit, the food we make, our wellbeing and life with animals.

Our name comes from the Japanese word erabu: to choose. We believe better choices begin with attention, context and the freedom to stay curious.
